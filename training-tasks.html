GTM Training Tasks

Complete the exercises below in your assigned GTM workspace. For each task, define the measurement requirement, configure the tag and trigger, test in GTM Preview, validate the expected event and parameters, and document your QA evidence.

Training Rule: Do not publish changes unless instructed by your trainer. Build and QA all assigned work inside your trainee workspace.

Completion Checklist

Define

Measurement Plan

Document the business purpose, user action, event name, parameters, page scope, and expected GA4 destination.

Configure

GTM Implementation

Create clearly named tags, triggers, and variables inside the assigned trainee workspace.

Test

Preview and QA

Confirm positive and negative firing conditions, parameter values, event order, and duplicate-event prevention.

Validate

GA4 Verification

Confirm the expected event reaches the training GA4 property with the intended event name and parameters.

Document

QA Evidence

Capture GTM Preview evidence, expected-versus-actual results, known limitations, and any required follow-up.

Review

Trainer Signoff

Submit the completed workspace and documentation for review. Do not publish without trainer approval.
